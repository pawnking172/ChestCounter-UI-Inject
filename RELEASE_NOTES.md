**English** — ChestCounter build 1.5.45. **Check for updates** follows this repository, so this build arrives through the button.

**What's new in 1.5.45**
- **Events tab:** a dedicated list of every important timed event of the hunt, with what it is, what it gives and how it helps you get the Pirate Shop token. Each card shows live/next status with a per-second countdown (only the small label changes, never the whole page), the next four times in your local/UTC and 12/24-hour settings, and a source link (Polygon).
- Honest token facts: the token is a golden-chest find (1 in 137,500 chests) that makes the whole Item Shop free for 14 days, twice (2026 and 2027). **Treasure Hour is the only event that raises the token odds** — 1/91,667 at ×1.5 and 1/68,750 at ×2. **Captain's Day does not change token odds**; it brings the new boss, Sprite and weapon and resets the weekly treasures.
- Event texts live in `content.json` (revision 4), so new events and corrected facts reach every counter without a new exe.
- Counts, replay analysis, report totals, prize table, map and overlay numbers are unchanged.
